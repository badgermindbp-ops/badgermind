What I do

From a busy clinic to a calm one.

Every clinic is different, but the leaks are usually the same: calls, bookings, no-shows, treatment plans, pending payments and check-up recalls. I start with your numbers and your routines, not with software.

🧭

Streamline the operation

I map how enquiries, bookings, visits, treatment plans and payments really run in your clinic. Then I write one-page routines, so any team member can follow them, even on a busy or short-staffed day.

📅

30-60-90 day business plan

One change at a time. Foundations in the first 30 days (missed calls, empty chairs), money in the next 30 (treatment plan follow-ups, billing, dues), growth in the last 30 (recall, reviews, stock).

📱

Social media content

Posts and reels in simple words: what a treatment involves, aftercare, common myths and patient questions. Educational, with no promised results, and always for you to check against your council's advertising rules.

📍

Google business management

I set up and manage your Google Business Profile: correct name, map pin and hours, WhatsApp button, photos, services, and a kind, honest way to ask for reviews and reply to unhappy ones.

🌐

Website creation

A fast, mobile-friendly clinic website with your treatments, doctors, location, WhatsApp button and an enquiry form, so patients who find you can reach you in one tap.

📸

Instagram audits

I review your profile, bio, highlights, posts and DMs to see why followers aren't becoming appointments, then give you a fix list ranked by impact.

🛠️

Custom tool development

If WhatsApp Business and a Google Sheet truly can't fix a workflow, I scope and build a tight custom tool around it, such as a recall tracker or a treatment plan follow-up tool. Often the honest answer is "not yet".
